Map credits

GrandStrategy combines geographic sources into a simplified game world. Elevation is cropped, resampled, projected and shaded; land cover and waterways are adapted for gameplay.

  • © OpenStreetMap contributors. Waterway data via Geofabrik, under the Open Database License 1.0.
  • Natural Earth public-domain physical geometry and Natural Earth II land cover.
